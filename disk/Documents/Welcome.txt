Welcome to Bevel OS
===================

This is a small desktop operating system running on an ESP32-S3
microcontroller: two cores at 240 MHz, 8 MB of PSRAM, a 640x480
VGA picture in 64 colours made by six resistors.

Things to try:

  * Press F1 for the list of keyboard shortcuts. Everything works
    without a mouse.
  * Open Files and look around this drive.
  * Edit this file and save it with Ctrl+S.
  * Start a game from the Bevel menu, under Programs.

The drive you are looking at lives in the board's own flash chip.

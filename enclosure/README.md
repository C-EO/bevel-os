# ESP32 VGA OS enclosure

Generated by `make_enclosure.py` (Blender 4.3, headless). Edit `P` at the top of the script, then:

    /Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --python enclosure/make_enclosure.py

Outputs land in `enclosure/out/`. Then, to rebuild the combined lid + spare parts plate:

    /Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --python enclosure/make_plate.py

## Assembly notes

- **Two-part box:** the split is 16 mm in from the rear wall. A rib on each side wall straddles
  it; drive the 4 joint screws from inside the body into the panel's pilot holes. The lid's four
  screws tie both halves together as well. If a port needs adjusting, only `rear_panel` is reprinted.
- **ESP32:** lies component side up on two rails (4 mm high, so the wires soldered under the
  board fit), COM port (the one next to pins 19/20) lined up with the COM window. Push it
  against the wall, then put the bridge on the two far-end bosses: the WiFi module's
  overhanging antenna end sits in the bridge's notch, the arms rest on the PCB corners. Slide
  the bridge toward the wall until the notch's back face touches the module end (slots allow
  +-3 mm), then tighten both M3 x 8. Plugging a cable in can't push the board back now.
- **USB-C breakouts:** slide each board onto its shelf with the receptacle facing the wall, then
  screw the clamp bar across the two cradle walls. The bar presses on the receptacle's metal
  shell, and its tooth drops into the bare strip between the receptacle and the V/D-/D+/G pads.
  Push the bar toward the wall until the tooth touches the back of the receptacle (slots allow
  +-1.5 mm), then tighten. The board can't slide into the box when a plug goes in.
- **VGA:** push the internal cable's plug out through the VGA hole from the inside, so the whole
  plug body (and its thumbscrews) is outside. Slide the clamp block under the cable on the floor
  pad behind the hole, put the cap on top and screw both down (2 x M3 x 20); the grooves grip the
  8 mm cable. Slip the cover plate over the cable from below (it has a slot) and screw it to the
  wall (2 x M3 x 10). Outside, the plug is male and the VGA-to-HDMI adapter's input is male too,
  so a female-female coupler goes between them.
- **VGA with the gender changer (current setup):** the internal cable's plug stays inside the box
  and mates through the hole with a female-female mini gender changer. Put the changer's body in
  the cage frame's pocket (inner D shell through the frame's window), hold the frame against the
  wall with the inner side through the VGA hole, plug the internal cable onto it from inside and
  tighten its thumbscrews, then put the cap on and drive both M3 x 20 through cap and frame into
  the pilots beside the hole. The body is clamped between the two 2 mm lips. If the body is
  thinner than ~6.2 mm it can rattle front to back: set `cpl_body_t` to the measured value.
- **Perfboard (70 x 30 mm):** drill four 3.2 mm holes 5 mm in from the edges, in a
  60 x 20 mm rectangle, to match four standoffs of the 20 mm grid (5 mm tall).
- **Zip-tie anchors:** one behind the VGA coupler (strain relief), one behind the ESP32.
